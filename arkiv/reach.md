# Reach

Poros is for trading communities that want to work with the reasoning behind a trade, not just react to a buy or sell call.

A creator can share a strategy. A member can inspect its rules, test a change, and publish a new version. The discussion stays connected to something both people can open and understand.

## Who we are building for

Our starting audience is traders who already exchange ideas in group chats and creator communities. Their next step is often manual: interpret a post, work out its assumptions, and recreate the rules somewhere else. Poros brings that review and testing into the same workflow as the conversation.

For creators, it connects public strategies and discussion with optional 30-day memberships for exclusive messages and strategy attachments. Members can inspect a creator's retained public versions and available trade history before deciding whether to subscribe. Membership pays for content access; it does not activate copy trading.

Developers are a second audience. Arkiv's public records let them build alternative readers, community tools, or strategy explorers without depending on our frontend.

## The first useful session

1. Browse a [community](https://poros-theta.vercel.app/community) and find a strategy worth examining.
2. Open its published rules in the [builder](https://poros-theta.vercel.app/). For a written strategy, use 0G inference to propose supported rules, then review the validated result.
3. Adjust the conditions and risk limits, and backtest with virtual funds.
4. Bring the reasoning back to the discussion, or publish a revision or fork with its source attached.

Public browsing needs no wallet. Publishing needs a wallet and Rabdi gas; written-strategy interpretation needs the user's 0G key and incurs inference costs. Reviewing and backtesting a public graph does not require a paid membership or permission to trade.

## A reason to come back

The reason to return is a strategy that keeps developing: a revised entry condition, a question from another member, a new test, or a creator explaining what changed. Version links make those conversations specific. People can compare the actual rules instead of trying to reconstruct them from old messages.

## How we plan to grow

After Mumbai, our proposed first step is to work with a small group of creators and their communities through the full publish, review, test, and revise cycle. We would measure whether a new member can complete it without help, whether they return to an updated strategy, and where they abandon the process.

Those observations would guide improvements to onboarding, smaller-screen editing, and member-content delivery before expanding to larger communities. The proposed reuse of unused 0G credits could support future agentic runs, but the current credit pool is a demo, not an adoption claim.

## Where we stand

Poros is a working demo, not yet a validated business. Existing public records are development activity; repeat usage and product-market fit have not been measured. Build checks, test results, and known limits are documented separately in the [verification report](../apps/web/VERIFICATION.md).
